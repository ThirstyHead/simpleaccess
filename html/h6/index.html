The <h6> Element

As defined in the HTML Living Standard, the <h6> element represents the lowest-level heading.

Visual Example:
This is a Rank 6 Heading
<h6>Lowest Level Heading</h6> View HTML Living Standard: Headings

The End of the Hierarchy

The H6 is the final level of the standard HTML heading hierarchy.

  • Structural Limit: There are no H7 or H8 tags. If you need further nesting, use lists or other grouping elements.
  • Consistency: Ensure that the H6 is used consistently for the lowest level of detail across your site.

WCAG Requirement: Info and Relationships

Success Criterion 1.3.1 (Level A): Information, structure, and relationships conveyed through presentation can be programmatically determined.

Even at the lowest level, headings provide essential structural landmarks for assistive technology.
