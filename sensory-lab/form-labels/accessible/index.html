What Good Looks Like: Semantic Bonding

In this accessible form, every <label> element is strictly bound to its corresponding <input> using matching for and id attributes.

🧪 Lab Experiment: Try hovering over the text "Full Name" below. Notice it changes color? Now, click the word "Full Name" instead of the box. Your cursor will jump directly into the input field! By using real labels, you've just doubled the clickable hit-area for users with motor impairments.
