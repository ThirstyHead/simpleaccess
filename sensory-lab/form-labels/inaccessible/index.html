The Inaccessible Twin: The Vanishing Context Trap

This form represents the "clean, minimalist" anti-pattern that causes 51% of forms to fail basic accessibility checks. It relies entirely on the HTML placeholder attribute instead of semantic <label> elements.

🧪 Lab Experiment: Click into the first box, type your first name, then hit the "Tab" key. Now, pretend you got distracted by a text message and look away. Look back at the screen: What is the empty box for? Email? Address? Phone number? Because there are no persistent labels, the context has vanished entirely.
