The Inaccessible Twin: The Keyboard Trap

Do not use your mouse. Click once anywhere on this page to ensure it has focus, then hit the Tab key. You will be able to tab into the scrollable terms box... and then what? You are completely trapped. The "buttons" are actually styled <div> tags, meaning the browser's native focus management completely ignores them. Even if you could reach them, pressing Enter does nothing.
