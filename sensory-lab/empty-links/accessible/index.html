What Good Looks Like: Hidden Context

🧪 Lab Experiment: Below is a standard e-commerce product card with icon-only action buttons. Visually, they are clean and minimalist. However, we've used two standard techniques to ensure a screen reader perfectly understands their purpose. Check the Screen Reader Simulator below to see how this translates.

Premium Reserve Beans

$19.99 | A rich, full-bodied espresso blend with notes of dark chocolate and toasted caramel.

🔊 Screen Reader Output when tabbing through the action bar:

"Share Premium Reserve Beans via Email, link."
[The screen reader ignores the SVG icon entirely and reads the `aria-label`.]

"Add to Favorites, button."
[The screen reader ignores the SVG icon entirely and reads the `sr-only` span.]

"Add to Shopping Cart, button."
[The user knows exactly what action this button will perform before they press Enter.]
