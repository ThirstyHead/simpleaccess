What Good Looks Like: Fluent Pronunciation

🧪 Lab Experiment: If you "View Source" on this page, you will see <html lang="en"> at the very top. But pay close attention to the French dish names below—they are wrapped in <span lang="fr">. Review the Screen Reader Simulator output to understand how this changes the auditory experience.

Le Petit Bistro

Soupe à l'oignon $12.00

A classic French onion soup based on meat stock and onions, served gratinéed with croutons and cheese on top.

Coq au Vin $28.00

Chicken braised with wine, lardons, mushrooms, and optionally garlic. Served with a side of roasted root vegetables.

Crème Brûlée $10.00

A rich custard base topped with a texturally contrasting layer of hardened caramelized sugar.

🔊 Screen Reader Output when reading the first menu item:

[Switches to French voice profile] "Soupe à l'oignon"
[Switches back to English voice profile] "Twelve dollars. A classic French onion soup based on meat stock..."

[The screen reader detects the `lang="fr"` attribute and temporarily loads its French pronunciation dictionary to say the dish name correctly. It then seamlessly reverts to its default English dictionary to read the description and price because the root `<html>` tag is set to "en".]
