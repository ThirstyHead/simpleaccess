The Sensory Lab: Fix the Six

Welcome to the Sensory Lab. This is a kinesthetic, experiential playground where you can feel the difference between "technically functional" and "humanly accessible." Explore our six digital twin experiments based on the top accessibility errors identified in the 2026 WebAIM Million report.

1. Low Contrast Text

WebAIM Impact: Found on 83.9% of home pages.

Experience the web through the lens of visual impairment. See how subtle grays on white backgrounds create invisible barriers for millions of users.

Launch Low Contrast Lab

2. Missing Alternative Text

WebAIM Impact: Found on 53.1% of home pages.

Turn off your monitor and navigate purely by sound. Discover what happens when crucial context is trapped inside an unlabeled graphic.

Explore Alt Text Lab

3. Missing Form Labels

WebAIM Impact: Affects 51% of form inputs.

Attempt to checkout of a shopping cart using only placeholder text. Feel the frustration when context disappears the moment you start typing.

Test Form Label Lab

4. Empty Links

WebAIM Impact: Present on 46.3% of home pages.

Navigate a page using a screen reader's links menu. Try to determine where you are going when every destination is read aloud as "click here."

Experience Empty Links Lab

5. Empty Buttons

WebAIM Impact: Affects 30.6% of home pages.

Interact with a site entirely by keyboard. Discover the trap of visually styled `div` elements that lack native button focus and semantics.

Launch Empty Button Lab

6. Missing Document Language

WebAIM Impact: Missing on 13.5% of home pages.

Listen as a screen reader attempts to read French content using an English pronunciation engine. Hear why declaring your HTML language is vital.

Examine Language Lab
